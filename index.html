AFK Town

Add AFK Town to your Home Screen, then play from the icon. Saves made here stay in this browser only.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open AFK Town from the new icon.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Add or Install, then open AFK Town from the new icon.
